PROJECT 03

Business Optimization

This project used linear programming to solve business problems involving limited resources and competing objectives.

What I Did

I created objective functions and constraints to model business scenarios involving profit maximization and cost minimization.

Approach

After identifying decision variables and constraints, I evaluated the feasible region and compared possible solutions to determine the optimal outcome.

What I Learned

The project showed me how quantitative models can turn real business limitations into structured decisions and help managers allocate resources more efficiently.

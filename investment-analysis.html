PROJECT 02

Investment Portfolio Analysis

This project examined investment opportunities by comparing both return potential and risk across different assets.

What I Did

I evaluated investment performance using measures such as expected return, volatility, variance, Sharpe ratio, and correlations between assets.

Approach

I compared individual investments and considered how combining assets could change the overall risk profile of a portfolio. Correlation analysis was especially useful for understanding diversification.

What I Learned

The project strengthened my understanding that investment decisions should not be based only on the highest return. Risk-adjusted performance and diversification are also important when evaluating a portfolio.

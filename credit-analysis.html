PROJECT 01

Corporate Credit Analysis

This project focused on evaluating a company's financial condition and business relationships from a credit perspective.

What I Did

I reviewed financial performance, revenue trends, customer and supplier relationships, payment cycles, and other business factors that could influence a company's ability to repay debt.

Approach

Rather than looking only at financial statements, I considered how the company actually operated. This included examining its major customers and suppliers, cash conversion cycle, business concentration, and potential sources of credit risk.

What I Learned

The project helped me understand that strong credit analysis requires combining financial data with an understanding of how a business generates and manages cash.
